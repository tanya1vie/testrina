ARCHITECTURE AND VIDEOGAMES

Intersecting Worlds

CONTEXT

Book published with Routledge

COLLABORATORS

Vincent Hui, Ryan Scavnicky

ROLE

Co-editor, chapter author, interview conductor

This book explores and affirms the emergent symbiosis between videogames and architecture, with insights from a diverse range of disciplines. Contributors from architecture and the videogame industry examine how games have brought the built environments of the past to wider audiences, heightened awareness of today’s urban context, and inspired possible futures for architecture.

Videogames have rapidly transitioned from cultural novelty to architectural prophet over the past 50 years. The book asks how a dominant interactive medium might influence the architecture of tomorrow.
