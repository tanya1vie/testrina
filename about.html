About Tatiana Estrina

I’m a designer creative coder ballroom dancer ceramicist nail artist problem solver urban sketcher reader cat mom .



Currently an Industrial Designer at Amazon Robotics . Previously, I was a design intern at Tesla and architectural designer at PARTISANS.

Studied architecture at Toronto Metropolitan University before crossing the border to study Architecture and Computer Science at MIT. My work now lives at the intersection of spatial design, human behavior, and emerging tools— especially wherever hardware, software, and bodies collide.
