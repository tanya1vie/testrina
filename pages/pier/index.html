TRANS-PIER

Where entertainment meets travel

A transportation hub that becomes a destination by combining cruise, ferry, cargo, and air travel with the history of the American pleasure pier.

CONTEXT

ACSA / AISC Steel Design Competition

LOCATION

Atlantic City, New Jersey

SOFTWARE

Rhinoceros 3D, Grasshopper + Pufferfish, V-Ray, Photoshop, Illustrator

RECOGNITION

2nd place, Open Category

The Trans-Pier turns Atlantic City’s dependence on tourism and transportation into an asset. It emphasizes the experience of people moving through the hub, letting them become participants in other travelers’ journeys.

The pleasure pier has been an icon of American summer retreats since the early 1900s. This proposal reinterprets that model for visitors enjoying the boardwalk and for those in transit.
