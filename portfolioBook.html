THE ARCHITECTURE PORTFOLIO GUIDEBOOK

Book Design and Assembly

This book, written by Professor Vincent Hui, guides architecture students in assembling a portfolio, both for applications to work and academic endevors. My role was to aid in the collection of imagery, assembly of said imagery, and keeping track of permission from 50+ sources. In addition I created many images and diagrams, created the cover design and copyediting design, helped transcribe and edit interviews, and wrote the appendices.
