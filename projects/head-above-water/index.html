HEAD ABOVE WATER

Interactive Data Visualization

Head Above Water is an interactive exploration of Boston’s historical land-making and contemporary flood risk. The project helps prospective residents compare shoreline change, property activity, and exposure to future flooding.

CONTEXT

Interactive Data Visualization

LOCATION

Boston, Massachusetts

SOFTWARE + TOOLS

D3.js, Mapbox GL JS, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

COLLABORATORS

Danny Griffin, Habin Park, Haidar El Haq

Understanding a city made from water

Boston’s shoreline has shifted continuously over the past three centuries. Neighborhoods created through land reclamation now face a changing climate and uneven exposure to coastal flooding.

The original interactive prototype combined historical maps with real-estate and flood-risk datasets. Its migration now preserves the project as a stable case study without relying on missing third-party map assets or private access tokens.
